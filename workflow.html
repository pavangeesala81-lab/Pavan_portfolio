Stage-by-Stage Methodology

STAGE 01 - 03

Strategic Alignment & Concepting

Analyzing technical engineering, healthcare, or corporate requirements. Defining visual tone, message hierarchy, and design thinking framework.

STAGE 04 - 07

AI Previsualization & Video Generation

Rapid prototyping using LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude) for script refinement, followed by AI storyboard previs and video generation with Google Veo 3, Kling AI, and Higgsfield.

STAGE 08 - 09

3D Modeling & Motion Graphics

Building high-precision 3D assets in Blender and compositing kinetic typography, UI overlays, and visual effects in After Effects.

STAGE 10 - 12

Editing, Sound Design & Broadcast Delivery

Pacing in Premiere Pro, cinematic color grading in DaVinci Resolve, spatial sound design, and multi-platform format export.
